NOTES:
Option 2: For the Red 2-Pin Main Power Plug (JST-RCY 2.50mm)

The red JST plug uses a 2.50mm pin spacing and usually connects via a wire-to-wire housing, but on a PCB, you will use a standard 2-pin JST header.
1. In the Schematic Editor (Symbol):
	• Press A and search for Conn_01x02_Pin.
2. In the PCB Editor (Footprint):
	• Assign the footprint library: Connector_JST.
	• Search for: JST_RCY or use a standard JST_XH_B2B-XH-A if you are splicing the wires into a matching white-style header on your board.
Pin Mapping for the Schematic:
• Pin 1: GND (Black wire)
• Pin 2: VCC / +7.4V (Red wire)
